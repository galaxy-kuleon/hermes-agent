## Problem Found

Both HK registered-design e-form PDFs had:

```json
{
  "source_tables_detected": 12,
  "docx_native_tables": 28,
  "docx_content_tables": 17,
  "docx_layout_tables": 6,
  "content_table_count_delta": 5,
  "failure_score": 37
}
```

The false content-table overcount came from 1-row/2-col form furniture and text fields being classified as semantic tables:

- title/banner: `Application for registration of designs Registered Designs Ordinance (Cap. 522)`
- continuation of general notes: `Either Chinese or English can be used as the language of proceedings...`
- long privacy block: `Use of Personal data...`
- note blocks: `Note 3...`, `Note 4...`
- long form text field: `The features of the design for which novelty is claimed...`

## Fix Implemented

Updated `classify_docx_table()` in `scripts/pdfdocx5.py`:

```python
form_noteish = any(tok in low for tok in [
    "general notes",
    "language of proceedings",
    "please complete the form",
    "use of personal data",
    "personal data is voluntary",
    "application for registration of designs",
    "registered designs ordinance",
]) or bool(re.match(r"^note\s*\d+\b", low))

form_text_fieldish = rows == 1 and cols <= 1 and any(tok in low for tok in [
    "features of the design",
    "novelty is claimed",
    "confidential disclosure",
])
```

If a one-row table matches these form furniture/text-field rules, classify it as:

```text
layout_or_furniture
```

not `content_table`.
